Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

Where do you source your products from?

Every product is hand-picked from Lahore's trusted wholesale markets — Hall Road and Azam Market — and checked for quality before it's listed on our site.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours and typically arrive within 2-4 working days, depending on your city.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan for all orders.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, if a product arrives damaged or doesn't match the description, you can request a return or exchange within 3 days of delivery.

Are the products original / genuine?

We're transparent about our sourcing — products are wholesale market items, not OEM-branded originals, but every piece is quality-checked before it ships.
